Who We Are

Made for Moments Worth Celebrating

At ASTANA Catering, we bring familiar Malaysian favourites to your special occasions through thoughtful preparation, inviting food presentations, and reliable catering service. From intimate family gatherings to corporate functions and wedding celebrations, we help make every occasion feel special.

Our Heritage

Founder’s Background & Experience

With a background in event planning and catering, the founder brings a refined understanding of event presentation, thematic styling and guest experience. This experience forms the foundation of the brand’s approach to creating distinctive food booths that combine classic Malaysian cuisine with elegant décor.

Driven by a passion for preserving traditional flavours while elevating the overall presentation, the founder aims to deliver a complete food booth experience where culinary quality, visual aesthetics and professional service come together seamlessly.
